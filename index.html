Une lettre

Dear Troy,

Happy birthday to one of the most wonderful men we have ever had in our lives. We don’t say it often enough, so we’ll say it here: we appreciate you, more than you know.

You live with a zest for life that is impossible to resist. You walk into a room and the whole evening gets brighter, louder, and a lot more fun.

The way you take care of Thierry, my best friend and your husband, is something beautiful to witness. He is lucky to have you, and so are we.

Whenever any of us needs anything, you’re already there, always willing and somehow always able to help.

You are a fabulous hairstylist and an even more fabulous friend. Your heart is the size of something infinite, and your kindness and generosity know no bounds.

So on your birthday, we want you to know two things. We appreciate everything you do for us. And we appreciate, even more, the amazing human being you are.

Joyeux anniversaire, mon cher.

With all our love, Kerry & Lisa
